# Ramble — Brand

The source of truth for what Ramble stands for and how we talk about it. Use it for the website, the App Store, social posts, release notes, and in-app copy. How Ramble looks and moves lives in [`design.md`](design.md). App Store fields live in [`app-store.md`](app-store.md).

## The belief

Your best thoughts show up when you're away from a screen: on a walk, in the car, at the stove, halfway through a run. Reaching for your phone to save one pulls you out of the moment, and then the phone keeps you.

Sending a thought to your agent usually starts a conversation. It replies, asks a follow-up, and now you're solving the problem on a screen instead of being where you are.

Ramble lets you keep the thought without leaving the moment. Say it to your watch, let it go, and get back to what you were doing. Nothing comes back. Your agent picks it up later and takes care of it. You only check in if it needs you.

**Ramble is a mindfulness app.** The product is less time on your phone and fewer conversations you didn't mean to start. A one-way hand-off from your watch to your agent is how it gets there.

## The name

A ramble is two things, and Ramble is both:

- **A walk with no destination.** The time when thoughts come. Ramble is built for it: phone at home, watch on your wrist.
- **Talking without polishing.** You don't have to compose anything. Say it the messy way it came to you. Your agent tidies it up.

Use both meanings in copy. "Go for a ramble" works. "Just ramble" works. Never use it to mean long or tedious: Ramble is for quick thoughts, said and gone.

## What Ramble is

The one-way line from your Apple Watch to your agent.

Tap your Apple Watch, talk, and put your arm down. The watch sends it to Ramble's server, which transcribes it, and the transcript goes to your agent, which picks it up later. No screen to read, no reply to wait for, and no signal needed. The watch is where you use Ramble. The phone holds your history and the settings, and it can stay in your pocket or at home.

**Category phrase** (use these exact words wherever a page or listing says what Ramble is): *One-way voice notes from your Apple Watch to your agent.*

**First mention:** "Ramble for Apple Watch", or "Ramble" next to ramble.watch. Other products are called Ramble, so the name alone isn't enough to find it.

## Positioning

**Say it. Let it go.**

Agents can take a voice message from a watch. What they can't do is stay quiet. Their apps are built for a conversation: they answer, ask follow-ups, and need a connection. Ramble is built for the opposite. One tap, one thought, nothing back, and it works with no signal. Your agent's own apps are for working things out together. Ramble is for the moments you don't want to.

Lead with what the person gets to do (say it and let it go), not with what the agent does. "Your agent picks it up later" belongs in body copy, where the word doing the work is *later*.

## The four promises

Every piece of copy should support at least one of these, and none should undercut them.

1. **Let it go.** No reply on your wrist, no follow-up question, no being pulled into solving it right now. The thinking can wait.
2. **Leave your phone behind.** Your watch is enough. It records with no phone and no signal, waits on your watch, and sends when it has a connection again.
3. **Stay in the moment.** A thought takes seconds. Tap, talk, done. Then you're back.
4. **Nothing sticks around.** No accounts. Audio is deleted as soon as the transcript is ready. Transcripts are deleted once your agent picks them up. We say exactly what's kept, where, and for how long.

## Who it's for

**Primary: people who already run their day through a personal agent and want to be less on their phone.** They walk, run, drive, cook, and parent. They have ideas and to-dos at awkward moments, and they're tired of every thought turning into a session on a screen. They don't want another inbox or another conversation. They want to say it and know it landed.

**Not for:** people who want a back-and-forth with their agent on the wrist, or an archive of recordings.

**Secondary: builders.** Developers and tinkerers who pipe voice into their own stack via webhook. Speak to them on the docs pages, not in the hero.

## Voice

Ramble sounds like a calm friend who respects your time.

- **Calm.** Unhurried. No urgency, no exclamation marks, no countdowns.
- **Short.** Short sentences, short words. If it can be said in five words, use five.
- **Plain.** Say what happens. "Your agent picks it up," not "seamless agent integration."
- **Honest.** Promise reliability, not transformation. Never claim more than the app does today.
- **Warm, not cute.** Friendly and human, without jokes, puns, or emoji in product copy.

### Copy rules

1. **Lead with the moment, then the mechanism.** "On a walk, a thought comes. Tell your watch." before "Ramble transcribes it and hands it to your agent."
2. **Write to "you."** Ramble is "it," never "we" in product copy. "We" is fine for privacy and pricing, where a person is accountable.
3. **Use concrete moments.** A walk, the car, the dentist reminder, oat milk on the list. Real examples beat abstract benefits.
4. **One idea per line.** Headlines are one thought. Body paragraphs are two or three sentences.
5. **Leave space.** Fewer words on the page is part of the brand. Cut before you add.
6. **Watch text is the tightest.** One or two words on the watch, icons and motion before text. See [`design.md`](design.md).
7. **Be exact about privacy.** Say what's stored, where, and for how long. Never vague comfort words.

### Words we like

moment, walk, say it, let it go, back to your day, nothing comes back, no reply, one-way, out of your head, messy is fine, leave your phone, no signal, quick, quiet, later, picks it up

### Words we avoid

| Avoid | Why | Say instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| productivity, hustle, crush, 10x, optimize | Ramble is about doing less on your phone, not doing more | "get back to your day" |
| never miss a thing, capture everything | Fear of missing out is the opposite of the brand | "keep the thought" |
| AI-powered, smart, revolutionary, game-changing, seamless | Hype that says nothing | say what it does |
| notes, note-taking | Ramble isn't a notes app; your agent is where things live | "thought," "what you say" ("notes" is fine in the price line, the category phrase, and on-screen states the specs set) |
| talk to your agent, chat, conversation (about Ramble) | Ramble is one-way; agents' own apps are the conversation | "say it," "hand it off," "let it go" |
| instantly, real-time | Agents pick up on their own schedule | "later," "when your agent checks in" |
| ramble on, rambling (as long or tedious) | Undercuts quick capture | "just say it," "messy is fine" |

## Message house

Use these as written, or start from them.

**Tagline:** Say it. Let it go.

**Hero:** Say it to your watch. Then let it go.

**Category phrase:** One-way voice notes from your Apple Watch to your agent.

**Search line** (App Store subtitle): Send thoughts to your AI agent

**Mindful line** (promo text, campaign): Thoughts now. Action later.

**One sentence:** Ramble for Apple Watch lets you say a thought and let it go: no reply to wait for, no signal needed, and your agent picks it up later.

**Short paragraph (~50 words):** Your best thoughts come when you're away from a screen. Ramble keeps them without pulling you back to one. Tap your Apple Watch, say it the messy way it came to you, and put your arm down. Nothing comes back. No signal? It sends later. Your agent picks it up and takes care of it. You only check in if it needs you.

**No reply. On purpose.** Most agents write back, then ask a follow-up, and you end up on your phone. Ramble doesn't reply. Say it, put your arm down, and keep going.

### By audience

- **Agent owners:** "Your agent's apps are for working things out together. Ramble is for the moments you don't want to. Say it to your watch, and your agent picks it up later. Connect with one message. No keys, no setup."
- **Privacy-minded:** "No accounts. Ramble's server is a relay, not an archive. Your audio is sent to a transcription provider and deleted as soon as the transcript is ready. Transcripts are deleted when your agent picks them up, or after 30 days. Your full history lives only on your iPhone."
- **Builders:** "Every transcript can POST to your own endpoint. Signed requests, automatic retries, full API docs."
- **Transcription:** "Accurate transcripts, with no model to pick. Messy is fine."

## Facts we can claim

Keep claims inside this list. If it isn't here, check the code before writing it.

- Records on the watch with no phone and no connection, and sends later. The watch uploads straight to Ramble's server, through the iPhone, Wi-Fi, or cellular.
- Transcribes on Ramble's server with Groq Whisper v3 Turbo. If Groq is overloaded or down, the note waits and is retried. Groq doesn't train on the audio. There is no model to choose.
- Connect an agent with a short single-use pairing code (like `K7QX-MP4D-HN3R`, expires in 10 minutes). No API keys or URLs.
- Copy says "your agent." Muse is the agent we build and test with, so name it where someone needs to know what's tested, and don't headline "works with any agent."
- No accounts. Privacy, in the words we use everywhere: "Ramble's server is a relay, not an archive. Your audio is sent to a transcription provider and deleted as soon as the transcript is ready. Transcripts are deleted when your agent picks them up, or after 30 days. Your full history lives only on your iPhone." Credentials are stored only as hashes.
- Usage stats, in the words we use everywhere: "Ramble also keeps basic usage stats for each note, like its length and whether it came from your watch or iPhone, under a random ID. Never your audio or your words." Never claim "no data collected" or "no analytics".
- Webhooks: one destination. Your iPhone sends a signed POST to any HTTPS endpoint once it has the transcript, with retries.
- Pricing, word for word: "Free to try. 25 notes free, then $14.99 once." No subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple.
- Never mention a usage number or monthly hours. The heavy-use threshold is an internal alarm for the operator, not a limit, and naming it makes people wonder if they're near it.

## What not to say

- Don't promise real-time delivery. Minutes of delay is normal and fine.
- Don't say "no servers" or "no data on our servers." The relay is a server. Say what it holds and when it deletes it.
- Don't say audio never leaves your device or never reaches the server. It does, and it's deleted as soon as the transcript is ready. Don't call Ramble on-device or offline transcription.
- Don't say "open source," "read the code," or "verify it yourself," and don't mention self-hosting.
- Don't call the free notes a "trial," and don't say "premium," "Subscribe," or "per month" next to a price. "Subscription" appears only in "No subscription."
- Don't claim a transcription model is better for noise or accents. No independent benchmark backs that.
- Don't promise MCP or dedicated ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok integrations, speaker labels, or end-to-end encryption until they ship. The Connect screen's "Works with Muse, Grok Bot, ChatGPT and more" is the one sanctioned agent list.
- Don't use fear-based privacy marketing or compare directly to other apps.

## What Ramble is not

- **Not a conversation.** Your agent doesn't reply on your watch. It's a one-way hand-off, and that's the point.
- **Not a voice memo.** A memo doesn't go anywhere. It sits there until you deal with it. Ramble sends the thought straight to your agent.
- **Not a notes app.** It captures voice and delivers text. Your agent or your own system keeps it.
- **Not an assistant.** No built-in LLM. Ramble feeds your agent; it isn't one.

When copy explains the difference, say "your agent's app" and "a voice memo" in general terms. Never name another app.

## Product principles

1. **Nothing ever gets lost.** Offline-first queues on watch and phone, clear status at every step. A thought recorded on a hike reaches your agent when you're back in range.
2. **Capture over organization.** Messy input beats no input. Your agent handles the structure.
3. **Less screen, not more.** Every feature should shorten the time someone spends looking at a device. If it adds a reason to look, question it.
4. **Hold as little as possible.** No accounts, audio deleted as soon as it's transcribed, transcripts kept only until delivery, with a stated maximum.
5. **Pluggable.** People choose their agent and their webhook. Ramble picks the transcription model, so there's nothing to tune.
6. **Ship and iterate.** Use it, notice what's missing, add that.
