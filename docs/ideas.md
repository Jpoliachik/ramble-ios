# Ideas for later

Possible future work. Nothing here is planned or promised, and none of it is built.

- **Speaker diarization**: opt-in. Deepgram has `diarize=true`; Groq Turbo doesn't do speaker labels.
- **Multiple language hints**: for people who codeswitch mid-recording.
- **On-device Apple Speech**: a possible opt-in fast follow.
- **Localization**: translate the app UI into more languages.
- **CarPlay**: hands-free recording in the car. Not supported today (the Siri phrases open the app on the phone). It needs Apple's approval for a CarPlay category that fits a voice-notes app, plus a CarPlay screen. Widen the marketing beyond the watch only once it exists.
