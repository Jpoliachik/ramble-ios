# Ramble — Brand Guide

## Brand Essence

**One line:** Tap. Talk. It's captured.

**What Ramble is:** An open-source voice capture app. Clean, reliable, private. It records what you say, turns it into text, and stays out of your way.

**What Ramble is NOT:** Productivity software. An AI assistant. A platform that wants your data.

## Brand Position

Ramble is a **utility**. A clean, useful tool that does one thing well.

It's open source, free, and stores nothing on any server. The codebase is the proof.

### Competitive Position

| Others | Ramble |
|--------|--------|
| "Record a voice memo" | "Capture a thought" |
| Sits on your phone in a folder | Lives on your wrist and your phone |
| You manage files | Your thoughts become text automatically |
| Requires setup, accounts, logins | Works immediately, no account needed |
| Stores your data on their servers | Your data stays on your device. Open source proves it. |
| Closed ecosystem | Webhook to pipe transcripts anywhere |

## Voice & Tone

- **Calm, not excited.** No exclamation marks. No hype. Quiet confidence.
- **Short, not clever.** "Tap. Talk. Done." Not "Revolutionize your thought capture workflow."
- **Human, not corporate.** Write like a person who found something useful.
- **Honest, not aspirational.** Don't promise transformation. Promise reliability.

### Examples:

| Instead of | Write |
|-----------|-------|
| "Supercharge your productivity with voice!" | "Talk. It writes it down." |
| "AI-powered transcription technology" | "Your words, as text, on your device." |
| "Join thousands of users" | (Don't say this. Let the product speak.) |
| "Never lose a brilliant idea again!" | "Your thoughts don't disappear anymore." |

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette

Clean and warm. Not techy, not startup-blue.

**Primary colors:**

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Stone | `#78716C` | Primary text, UI elements |
| Warm Sand | `#D6CFC7` | Backgrounds, cards |
| Sage | `#87A878` | Accents, success states, record button |
| Deep Forest | `#3D5A3E` | Headers, emphasis |
| Cream | `#F5F0EB` | Page backgrounds |

**Secondary / accent:**

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Terracotta | `#C67B5C` | Warm accents, alerts |
| Sky | `#8FAFCA` | Links, info states |
| Dusk | `#9B8FA0` | Muted secondary text |
| Bark | `#5C4B3C` | Dark mode text |

### Typography

- System fonts in-app (SF Pro). Clean, native.
- Avoid: monospace, thin weights, all-caps.

### Logo Direction

- Simple. Works at watch-complication size.
- A waveform that feels natural, not technical.
- The logo should be recognizable at any size.

>
> Ramble turns your voice into text, instantly. No account. No setup. Your words stay on your device.
>
> Record from your phone or your watch. Every thought gets transcribed and saved — ready to copy, search, or send anywhere via webhook.
>

### Keywords to own

- voice capture
- voice to text
- voice notes
- speech to text
- private voice recorder
- open source voice recorder

## Brand Don'ts

1. **Don't lead with technology.** "AI-powered" is not a selling point. "Your thoughts, captured" is.
2. **Don't use startup language.** No "disrupting," no "10x," no "game-changing."
3. **Don't overpromise.** "Capture your thoughts" is a promise we can keep.
4. **Don't hide behind marketing.** The open source code speaks for itself.
