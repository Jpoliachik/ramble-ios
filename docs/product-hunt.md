## Maker's first comment

Posted as Justin when the launch goes live. Product Hunt stores it as HTML (`<p>`, `<ul>`, `<strong>`); this is the same text.

> Hi Product Hunt, I'm Justin, and I made Ramble for Apple Watch.
>
> My best thoughts show up away from a screen: on a run, in the car, or on the floor playing with my kids. Capturing it meant reaching for my phone, always glued longer than I wanted.
>
> Sending it to my agent was worse. It would write back with a question, and suddenly I was working through the problem on my phone instead of playing with my kids.
>
> Ramble is the one-way line. Tap your Apple Watch, say it, then go back to your life. Nothing comes back. You stay present. Your agent picks it up later.
>
> - **No reply, on purpose.** No follow-up questions on your wrist.
> - **Leave your phone behind.** It queues up messages with no phone and no signal, and sends when it has a connection.
> - **Connect with one message.** A short single-use pairing code. No API keys or URLs.
> - **Security-first system design.** No accounts. Ramble's server is a relay, not an archive. Your audio is sent to a transcription provider and deleted as soon as the transcript is ready. Transcripts are deleted when your agent picks them up, or after 30 days. Your full history lives only on your iPhone.
> - **For custom agent builders too:** every transcript can POST to your own HTTPS endpoint, signed, with retries.
>
> Free to try. 25 notes free, then $14.99 once. No subscription.
>
> I'd love to hear where your thoughts show up, and what you'd want your agent to do with them.
