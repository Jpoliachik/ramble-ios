## Description

```
Your Apple Watch is all you need. Say the thought, then get back to what you were doing. Your agent handles it. No phone, no app, no waiting for a reply.

A to-do, an idea, something to look up later. Messy is fine. Your agent picks it up within a few minutes and deals with it. No phone out, no reply to wait for.

SETUP
In Settings, tap Connect agent to get a short code. Tell your agent "I downloaded Ramble, connect it" and paste the code. That's it.

PRIVACY
- No accounts.
- With on-device transcription, audio never leaves your iPhone. Only the text goes to your agent.
- Text is deleted once your agent picks it up, or after 30 days if it never does.
- Disconnect any time.

TRANSCRIPTION
Apple Speech transcribes on your iPhone. An optional upgrade adds cloud transcription for sharper results with accents, jargon, or noise. You never need it.

WEBHOOKS
Ramble can also send each transcript to any HTTPS endpoint.

Ramble is a one-time purchase. Requires an Apple Watch paired with an iPhone to record on the watch.
```

## What's New

```
Ramble now hands what you say to your agent. Tap Connect agent in Settings, paste the code to your agent, and start talking. Transcripts are deleted once your agent picks them up. Webhooks work as before.
```
